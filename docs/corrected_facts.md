# Corrected-facts crib sheet

The original brainstorming notes contained several factual errors. These were
fact-checked against NOAA, USGS, NC DMF, UNC/Duke, and APNEP sources. **Use the
corrected version in any proposal or talk** — several are load-bearing.

| Claim in the notes | Corrected |
|---|---|
| Menhaden killed in Chicago, 1964, by high temperature | **Alewife** (*Alosa pseudoharengus*) mass die-off, **Lake Michigan / Chicago shoreline, 1967**. Menhaden are Atlantic/Gulf — absent from the Great Lakes. |
| ~20 species extinct in the Great Lakes | Great-Lakes-wide: **26 fish extirpated from ≥1 lake, 4 globally extinct** (DFO checklist). "~20" only as a loose basin-wide figure. |
| Sturgeon fishery collapsed in the 1960s–70s | Lake sturgeon collapsed **~1880s–1920s** (caviar/overfishing); ~1% of historic abundance today. The 1960s–70s is when **blue pike** went extinct and the last deepwater ciscoes were lost. |
| Cisco/whitefish lost mainly to logging siltation | Primary drivers were **overfishing + invasives** (sea lamprey, alewife, smelt); siltation/habitat loss were contributing, not the lead cause, and not attributed to logging. |
| 2nd-largest estuary in the **world** | **"2nd-largest estuarine complex in the lower 48"** (APNEP). Separately, **Pamlico Sound is the largest *embayed* estuary in the world** — keep distinct. |
| "195 trail survey ignores salinity/rainfall/turbidity/temperature" | **FALSE and load-bearing.** Program **195** is the *Pamlico Sound trawl survey* — **stratified-random**, June & September since 1987 — and it **records** surface/bottom temperature, salinity, DO, secchi (turbidity), and precipitation at every grid. The real critique is **coarse temporal resolution (twice/yr) + under-use of the covariates**, not non-measurement. (A fixed-station grid description matches **Program 120**, the Estuarine Trawl Survey.) |
| NC #2 in hog production | **#3 in total hog inventory** (Iowa, Minnesota ahead); ~#2 in the breeding herd. State the metric + year (USDA NASS 2024–25). |
| Hogs = #1 nutrient-pollution source in NC | **Poultry has overtaken swine** (~3× the N, ~6× the P per NC DEQ 2017). |
| Croaker/spot "crashed ~97%" | That is a **commercial-landings** decline, not abundance — NC fishery-independent surveys show croaker and spot **remain among the most abundant** estuarine fish. Always distinguish landings from abundance. |
| Drought pushes crabs into freshwater and they die | **Backwards:** drought *raises* salinity; higher salinity correlates with *lower* crab abundance and faster *Hematodinium* spread. |
| Urchins with lesions as an NC stress signal | Urchin wasting belongs to the **Gulf of Maine** green-urchin system; NC has no significant urchin fishery — move out of the NC framing. |
| FerryMon = clean continuous 20-yr record; "Fairymon" | **FerryMon** (UNC Paerl Lab). **NOT continuous**: funding gap after ~2016; Pamlico routes down since 2019; Neuse route down ~2021. Treat as a stretch goal, not a dependency. |
| Cape Hatteras NERR (Beaufort) | **No Cape Hatteras NERR.** NC NERR components: Currituck Banks, **Rachel Carson** (Beaufort), Masonboro Island, Zeke's Island. |

## Key correct anchors to cite

- **Degree-days for fish:** Neuheimer & Taggart (2007), *Can. J. Fish. Aquat. Sci.* —
  GDD explained >92% of pre-maturation length-at-day variance across 9 species.
- **The AI Scientist** (process methodology): Lu et al. (2026), *Nature* 651, 914–919.
- **Albemarle–Pamlico**: APNEP (2nd-largest estuarine complex in the lower 48).
